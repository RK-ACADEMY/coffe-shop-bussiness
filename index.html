Our Philosophy

A dedication to the journey from seed to cup.

Founded in 2021 by a collective of passionate baristas and roasters, Aura & Roast was born out of a desire to create a sanctuary where coffee is not merely a morning utility, but a mindful ritual.

Every single bean in our shop is sourced from high-elevation family farms across Colombia, Ethiopia, Guatemala, and Sumatra. We pay up to 40% above fair-trade prices, ensuring our farmer partners can invest in organic cultivation and biodiverse soil health.

The Aura Method

The Art of Our Craft

Four deliberate steps that transform green coffee cherries into an extraordinary sensory experience.

01

Ethical Sourcing

We travel directly to volcanic, high-elevation micro-farms to hand-select specialty grade cherries.

02

Micro-Lot Roasting

Custom curve profiles tailored to each bean's density to highlight delicate florals and deep caramels.

03

Precision Extraction

Reverse-osmosis mineral-balanced water paired with temperature and flow-rate profile control.

04

Mindful Serving

Presented in handmade ceramic vessels to preserve aromatic nuances from first sip to last drop.
